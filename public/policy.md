- Email address;

- Phone number, where requested;
- Login method;

- Google account information provided through Google Sign-In;

- Profile biography;
- Name or display name;

If you use Glenn’s messaging features, we may process:

When users participate in tournaments or related activities, we may collect:

Tournament results, team names, player names, rankings, and similar competitive information may be publicly displayed.

### 3.7 Location Information

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, Glenn may collect:

* Approximate location;
* Precise device location;
* Manually selected city, state, region, or country;
* Location coordinates required for nearby-player, discovery, or map features.

Device-based location is collected only when the relevant permission has been granted and a feature requiring location is used.

Users may withdraw location permission at any time through their device settings. Some nearby-player, discovery, map, and other location-based features may not function correctly without location access.

If a user grants location permission and uses Glenn's nearby-player or location-based features, their location may automatically be used to make their profile discoverable to nearby users. Glenn may display an approximate area, distance, or nearby status based on the user's device location. Glenn does not intentionally display exact GPS coordinates or a full residential address to other users.

Users will also be able to disable location visibility through an in-app privacy setting. This control is planned to be available by the public production release of Glenn. Disabling location visibility or revoking location permission may prevent nearby-player and other location-based features from functioning.

- UPI ID;
- Account holder name;
- Bank account details, where required;
- Payment or transaction reference;

- Payout status;

Payment information is generally optional and is collected only when a user uses a feature that requires payment or payout processing.

Glenn does not intend to collect full payment-card numbers, CVV codes, internet-banking passwords, UPI PINs, or OTPs. Users should never send such credentials through posts, messages, forms, or support channels.

Banks, UPI applications, payment processors, and financial institutions may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the Service, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, maintaining security, and complying with legal obligations.

Retention may depend on the type of information.

### 12.1 Account and Profile Information

Account and profile information is generally retained while the account remains active and until the account is deleted.

### 12.2 User Content

Posts, comments, media, and other content may remain until:

- The user deletes the content;
- The account is deleted;
- Glenn removes the content;
- The content is no longer needed for the Service.

Content copied or shared independently by another user may remain outside Glenn’s control.

### 12.3 Messages

Messages may be retained to provide conversation history and operate messaging features.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or irreversibly de-identify associated message data where required and technically feasible, subject to another user’s rights, legal obligations, security needs, and valid dispute records.

### 12.4 Tournament Records

Results, rankings, rule-enforcement records, transaction records, and tournament evidence may be retained where needed to maintain competition history, investigate disputes, prevent repeated abuse, or fulfil legal and financial obligations.

### 12.5 Payment and Delivery Records

Payment, payout, transaction, shipping, and delivery records may be retained for accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and compliance with applicable law.

### 12.6 Diagnostics and Security Logs

API error records, diagnostics, authentication logs, and security logs may be retained for a limited period necessary to diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

### 12.7 Backups

Deleted information may remain temporarily in restricted backups until the backups are overwritten or securely deleted according to our normal backup cycle.

### 12.8 Required Retention

We may retain limited information after account deletion where reasonably necessary for:

- Legal compliance;
- Fraud prevention;
- Security;
- Dispute resolution;
- Enforcement of agreements;
- Financial or transaction records.

Where possible, retained information will be restricted, minimised, or de-identified and will not be used for unrelated purposes.
